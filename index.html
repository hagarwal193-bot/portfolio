// 01 — ABOUT

An HR generalist who treats operations like infrastructure.

I'm Harsh Agarwal, a Human Resource and Admin Executive based in India, currently driving HR and facilities operations at Ashish Polymers. Across four organizations since 2020, I've built my practice around one idea: HR runs better when it's documented, measured, and repeatable — not improvised.

My day-to-day spans end-to-end talent acquisition, structured onboarding, HRMS-driven attendance and payroll, PF/ESIC compliance, and the reporting layer that turns raw workforce data into decisions leadership can act on. I'm equally comfortable closing an offer, reconciling a salary sheet, or building the Excel calculator that makes the next cycle faster.
